3D Face Selection

1. Select surfaces — double-click a flat surface, shift+double-click to add more (each gets its own color). 2. Set connections — for surfaces that aren't directly touching, choose which corners join them (or leave it automatic). 3. Unwrap — flatten the selection into a 2D outline.
Pick connection corners:
  1. Click a corner marker on the left surface (colored by which original click it came from).
  2. Click the matching corner on the right surface — draws a green connecting line.
  3. Repeat once more, picking a second pair of matching corners.
  4. After 4 clicks (2 pairs) it connects automatically. "Cancel" backs out anytime.
